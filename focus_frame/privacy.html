Privacy Policy

Focus Frame

Last updated: September 30, 2026

TripleEdge Studio operates Focus Frame (package: com.focusframe.app). We do not sell your personal information.

1. Summary

  • No account required. Focus timers, session history, and app settings are stored on your device.
  • Free users may see Google AdMob ads: a labeled banner at the top of the screen, occasional full-screen interstitials, and optional rewarded videos when you choose to unlock a focus session.
  • We use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for ad consent and in-app ad privacy choices where required.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect anonymous usage and crash diagnostics to improve stability.
  • Optional one-time Pro purchase removes ads.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob only (no third-party ad mediation networks in this app). AdMob may use the Android advertising ID and other device data to serve, measure, and limit ads, subject to your consent choices.

  • Banners appear at the top of the main experience, labeled "Advertisement", and are hidden during the splash screen and for Pro users.
  • Interstitials may show after you leave the splash screen and take a meaningful action (for example navigation or finishing a focus session). They are limited to a small number per app session and are spaced with a cooldown so they do not appear on every tap.
  • Rewarded ads play only when you tap to watch a video (for example to start a focus session). They are never auto-played.

You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and use in-app Ad privacy choices when UMP shows them.

Our AdMob publisher ID is listed in the site app-ads.txt file (pub-7250491053459839) for authorized digital sellers verification.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time Pro purchase (focusframe_pro) via Google Play removes ads. Google processes payment; we do not store card or bank details.

4. Permissions

The app requests only what it needs:

  • Internet and Network state — load ads, Firebase, billing, and consent forms.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — used by AdMob for free users; not used for Pro users who have ads removed.
  • Google Play Billing — optional Pro purchase and restore.
  • Vibrate — optional haptic feedback when a focus session completes (device only).

We do not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or SMS.

5. Data on Your Device

  • Completed focus sessions and insights are stored locally (on-device database). We do not upload your session history to our servers.
  • Preferences (appearance, timer behavior, Pro status) use on-device storage.

6. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration unless you email us for support.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.

7. Third Parties

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
  • Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

8. Children

Focus Frame is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will change when we do.
