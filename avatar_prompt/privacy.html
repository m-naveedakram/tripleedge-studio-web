Privacy Policy

Avatar Prompt

Last updated: September 30, 2026

TripleEdge Studio provides Avatar Prompt (package: com.tripleedgestudio.avatarprompts). We do not sell your personal information. This policy explains what the Android app uses and how information is handled.

1. Summary

  • No account is required. Avatar ideas, drafts, and saved prompts are stored on your device (local database and preferences).
  • The app does not send your prompt text to a TripleEdge Studio server or run in-app AI image generation.
  • Free users may see Google AdMob ads: a labeled top banner, occasional full-screen interstitials at natural breaks, and optional rewarded videos when you choose them.
  • We use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for ad consent where required, including in-app ad privacy choices when applicable.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics help us understand usage and fix crashes.
  • Optional Google Play Billing one-time Pro purchase removes ads.

2. Information on Your Device

The app stores avatar specifications, generated prompt text, saved prompts, drafts, theme and builder preferences, and Pro entitlement flags locally. Export saves a file you choose on your device. Copy places text on the system clipboard. Share sends text only to an app you select. When you paste a prompt into ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney, or another tool, that service handles the text under its own policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob only (no third-party ad mediation networks in this app). AdMob and its SDK may process:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID on supported devices), subject to your Android and Google ad settings
  • IP address (which may imply approximate location), device type, OS version, language, and app interaction with ads
  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention data needed to deliver and measure ads

Banners are shown at the top of main screens with an "Advertisement" label, away from primary buttons. Interstitials appear only after meaningful actions (for example opening the builder or viewing a result), not on the splash screen, with frequency limits per session. Rewarded ads play only when you tap to watch them (for example to unlock premium art styles for the session). Pro users do not see banners or interstitials.

You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and use in-app Ad privacy choices when UMP requires it.

4. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Firebase Analytics may collect app opens, session data, device and OS information, and advertising identifiers where available. We use this in aggregate to improve the app. Analytics is not used to upload your prompt content as custom events.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs and stack traces tied to an app installation to diagnose stability issues. Crash reports are disabled in debug builds where configured.

5. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time Pro purchase (avatarprompt_pro) through Google Play removes ads and unlocks Pro features. Google processes payment; we do not receive or store your full card or bank details. Purchase tokens are used to verify and acknowledge ownership.

6. Permissions and Network

The app declares and uses the following on Android:

  • Internet — ads, analytics, crash reporting, Play Billing, and consent services
  • Access network state — check connectivity before ad and billing operations
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — used by AdMob for free-tier advertising and measurement, where allowed on your device
  • Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING) — in-app purchases only

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, calendar, or precise location permissions. Prompt building and saved library work offline; ads, purchases, and consent flows need a network connection.

7. Your Choices and Retention

  • Clear app data: Android Settings → Apps → Avatar Prompt → Storage → Clear data (removes drafts, saved prompts, and preferences).
  • Uninstalling the app removes local data from your device.
  • Rewarded ads and interstitials require your action or eligible navigation; they are not played automatically on launch.

Email support messages are kept only as long as needed to respond. Google service data follows Google's retention policies.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us.

  • Firebase privacy information
  • Google Mobile Ads — Play data disclosure
