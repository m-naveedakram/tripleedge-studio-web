Privacy Policy

Icon Prompt Generator

Last updated: September 25, 2026

TripleEdge Studio provides Icon Prompt Generator (package: com.tripleedgestudio.iconprompt). This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

1. Summary

  • No app account is required. Prompt creation uses templates on your device, not a remote AI service.
  • Ideas, generated and saved prompts, the latest bonus pack, and preferences are stored locally.
  • The app uses Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Google Play Billing, which process information as explained below.

2. Prompts and Local Storage

The app stores your current idea, prompt text, selected options, saved prompts, latest rewarded bonus pack, theme preference, and cached purchase status in app storage. The app does not upload prompt text to a developer-operated server or send it as custom Firebase Analytics events.

Copy places text on the system clipboard. Share sends selected text to an app you choose. When you paste a prompt into an external AI tool, that tool handles it under its own privacy policy. Avoid including sensitive information in text you share.

3. Advertising

Google AdMob provides banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. Its SDK may collect IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain its services.

The optional Remove Ads purchase disables banners and automatic interstitials. Optional rewarded ads remain available when you choose to watch them.

4. Analytics

Google Analytics for Firebase collects usage information such as app opens and sessions, app-instance identifiers, device and operating-system information, and advertising identifiers where available. We use analytics to understand usage and improve the app. This information can be associated with an installation and is not necessarily anonymous. The current app does not include Firebase Crashlytics.

5. Purchases

Where available, the one-time remove_ads purchase is processed through Google Play Billing. The app uses purchase status and purchase tokens to recognize and acknowledge purchases and caches entitlement locally. Google processes payment details; the app does not receive your full card or bank details.

6. Permissions and Network Use

Internet and network-state access support ads, analytics, and Google Play services. Included Google SDKs may access advertising identifiers subject to Android settings and availability. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or precise GPS permissions. Prompt generation and saved prompts work offline; ads and purchases need connectivity.

7. Your Choices

You can manage advertising privacy controls in Android settings; names and availability vary by Android version. These controls do not necessarily stop all analytics or advertising data collection. Rewarded ads require your action. The current app does not provide an in-app analytics opt-out switch or an ad-consent preferences screen.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local data remains until deleted in the app where available, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. To clear local data, open Android Settings → Apps → Icon Prompt Generator → Storage → Clear data. This removes saved prompts and preferences.

Clearing local data does not delete Google Play transaction records or information already processed by Google services. Those records follow the providers' retention settings and policies. Contact us about access or deletion requests relating to information we control; we may need details to identify relevant records.

9. Support and Service Providers

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments. We use them to respond and retain them as needed to address your request and applicable obligations. Google services may process information outside your country. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

The app does not request a child's name or age to create prompts. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.
