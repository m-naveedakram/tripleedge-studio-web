Getting Started

  1. Enter an icon idea in Create.
  2. Choose styles, colors, shapes, backgrounds, and optional advanced details.
  3. Generate a text prompt, then copy, share, or save it.
  4. Paste the prompt into your preferred external AI image tool to create artwork.

Common Questions

  • Where is my image? This app creates text prompts, not images. An external image tool is needed.
  • Can I work offline? Prompt templates and saved prompts work offline. Ads and purchases need internet.
  • Restore Remove Ads: Open Settings → Restore purchases using the same Google Play account used to buy it. Availability depends on the product being offered through Google Play.
  • Why can I still watch a rewarded ad? Remove Ads disables banners and automatic interstitials; bonus rewarded ads remain optional.
  • No bonus pack: A reward is granted only when the ad service confirms completion. If an ad is unavailable, try again later. Use View latest bonus pack to reopen the most recent earned pack.
  • Missing saved prompts: Prompts are local to the app installation. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes them; there is no cloud restore.
  • Light or dark theme: Choose your theme in Settings.

Privacy and Data Removal

To clear local data, open Android Settings → Apps → Icon Prompt Generator → Storage → Clear data. This removes saved prompts and preferences, but does not erase Google Play transaction history or data already processed by Google services. See the Privacy Policy for details.
