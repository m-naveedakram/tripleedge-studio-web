OCR Text Scanner

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Help for OCR Text Scanner by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.ocrtextscanner.

Camera scanning

  • Choose the camera option, allow Camera permission, hold the document steady in good light and keep text in focus.
  • If permission was denied, open Android Settings → Apps → OCR Text Scanner → Permissions → Camera.
  • Crop or rotate the image when needed, then run OCR. Blurry, angled, handwritten or highly decorated text may produce inaccurate results.

Gallery and PDF import

Use image import to choose a photo through Android's picker. Use PDF OCR to select a readable, unprotected PDF through the document picker. The app does not need broad storage permission. Corrupt, encrypted, inaccessible or very large files may be rejected to protect stability.

Auto and manual OCR scripts

Auto is the default and evaluates the bundled Latin, Chinese, Devanagari, Japanese and Korean recognizers. If results are poor, select the expected script manually and retry. App interface language does not change which OCR scripts are supported; Arabic and Urdu OCR models are not included.

Batch scanning

Add several selected images or camera captures, review their order, remove unwanted pages and start processing. Batch OCR runs sequentially to limit memory use. For a memory error, retry with fewer pages or smaller images. Keep the result open until an important edit has been saved or exported.

Local history, export and speech

  • Use Save to keep a result in local History. You can rename, favorite, delete individual scans or clear History.
  • Export recognized text as TXT or PDF and choose a destination with Android's document picker. Shared and exported copies must be deleted separately.
  • Copy or share text using the available result actions. Text-to-speech depends on an installed Android voice; offline voice availability varies by device.

Offline OCR, ads and privacy choices

OCR recognition runs on your device and does not require uploading documents. Internet access can still be used for Google AdMob, Google UMP consent and Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics if you separately enable those optional diagnostic controls.

Open Settings → Privacy & data to review the independent Analytics and crash-reporting options. Both default to off. Where required and available, use Advertising privacy options there to revisit UMP choices. Scanning should remain available if consent or an ad cannot load.
