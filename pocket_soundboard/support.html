Pocket Soundboard

Need help with recording, adding sounds to a board, imports, exports, or backups? This page supports Android package com.tripleedgestudio.soundboard.

  • Import: open Sounds or Studio, choose Import audio, then pick an audio file in Android's document picker.
  • Recording: choose Record sound and grant microphone access when prompted.
  • Boards: create a board, open it, and use Add sounds to select sounds from your library.
  • Locked sounds: tap a locked sound, then choose Watch ad to unlock. The unlock is saved only after the rewarded ad confirms completion. If the ad is unavailable, try again later or use a free or imported sound.
  • Backups: use Settings → Export backup and keep the file somewhere safe; Restore backup replaces your current collection. Rewarded unlock choices are not included in manual collection backups.

If something fails, include your Android version, phone model, and the steps to reproduce. Do not attach private recordings unless you choose to.
