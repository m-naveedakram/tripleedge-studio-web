Khmer Keyboard

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Help for Khmer Keyboard by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.khmerkeyboard.

Enable and select the keyboard

Open the companion app's setup flow, use the Android control to enable Khmer Keyboard, then select it as the current input method. If it does not appear, check Android's keyboard/input-method settings. Device manufacturers may use different setting names. Use Android's keyboard switcher to return to another keyboard.

Khmer, English, emoji and appearance

Use the keyboard's language and symbol controls to change input modes. Open Emoji from the toolbar and use its categories or recent items. Appearance, keyboard size, sound and vibration are available in settings. The companion app supports English and Khmer localization.

Voice typing

  • Allow microphone access when requested, then start voice input using the microphone control. You can cancel a session.
  • Check microphone permission in Android Settings and that the phone has a working speech recognition service supporting Khmer or English.
  • The configured recognition provider may need internet access. Offline recognition is not guaranteed; unavailable languages or provider/network errors can prevent recognition.
  • Voice input is restricted in fields the app identifies as sensitive. Review recognized text before sending it.

Clipboard and personalized suggestions

Clipboard history and personalized learning are off by default. Enable them in settings if wanted. Use clipboard entry deletion or Clear history to remove saved clipboard items, and Clear Learned Words to remove personalized words. Turning a feature off stops future use/capture but does not automatically erase stored entries. Clearing keyboard history does not clear Android's system clipboard or another app's copies.

Pro availability, purchase and restore

Open the Pro/Remove Ads screen to see Google Play's current availability and localized price. If unavailable, use the retry/check-availability control and confirm a working internet connection and Play services. Checkout also needs the secure verification service; availability is not guaranteed while that service is unavailable or not yet configured.

If already purchased, use Restore with the purchasing Google Play account. Pending payments do not unlock Pro. Restoration automatically retries temporary failures while the app is active and refreshes on resume. Offline or unresolved restoration holds ads until it resolves. For payment or refund issues, use Google Play's purchase help; do not send us full purchase tokens or card information.

Ads and privacy controls

The typing keyboard is ad-free. Eligible dashboard banners require advertising consent/eligibility and disappear for verified Pro users. Use the advertising privacy option in settings when available. Analytics and crash reporting are separate optional switches, both off by default. See the Privacy Policy for details.
