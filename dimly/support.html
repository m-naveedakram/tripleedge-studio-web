Dimly — Screen Brightness

Need help with brightness, permissions, ads, or Pro? We're here.

Common fixes

  • Dimly stopped working — Open Settings → Permission status and re-grant overlay or notification permissions. Check battery optimization (Settings → Troubleshooting).
  • Screen too dark — Pull down the notification shade and use the Dimly slider, or open the app and raise brightness on the ring.
  • Overlay not showing — Grant "Display over other apps" in Android settings.
  • Restore Pro purchase — Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Ads / consent — See our Privacy Policy for AdMob and UMP details.
