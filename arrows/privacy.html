Privacy Policy

Arrows

Last updated: September 29, 2026

TripleEdge Studio operates Arrows (package: com.tripleedgestudio.arrows). We do not sell your personal information.

1. Summary

  • No account required. Puzzle progress stays on your device.
  • The free version may show Google AdMob ads: a banner at the top of play, an interstitial after you leave a finished level, and an optional rewarded video for one extra life.
  • A one-time purchase (arrows_remove_ads) removes ads.
  • We use Google UMP for consent where required.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for anonymous usage and crash diagnostics.

2. Advertising

We use Google AdMob only. Ads are labeled, kept away from play buttons, and never required to finish a puzzle. Rewarded videos are optional. AdMob may use advertising identifiers subject to your consent choices.

You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and use in-app Ad privacy choices when available.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time purchase arrows_remove_ads via Google Play removes ads. Google processes payment. We do not store card details.

4. Data we do not collect

  • We do not upload your boards, backups, or progress to our servers.
  • No account registration unless you email us for support.

5. Third parties

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
