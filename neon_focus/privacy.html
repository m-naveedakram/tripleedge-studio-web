Privacy Policy

Neon Focus Sleep

Last updated: September 27, 2026

TripleEdge Studio operates Neon Focus Sleep (package: com.tripleedgestudio.neonfocussleep). We do not sell your personal information.

1. Summary

  • No account required. Sleep sounds, alarms, and settings stay on your device.
  • Free users may see Google AdMob ads (banner and interstitial). Pro removes ads.
  • We use Google UMP for consent where required.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for anonymous usage and crash diagnostics.
  • If you do not open the app for a day, the app may show one local reminder notification. That reminder is scheduled on the device and is not sent from our servers.

2. Advertising

We use Google AdMob only (no third-party mediation networks in this app). AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device data to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices.

You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and use in-app "Ad privacy choices" when available.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time Pro purchase (neonfocussleep_pro) via Google Play removes ads. Google processes payment; we do not store card details.

4. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not upload your sound mixes, alarms, or practice history to our servers.
  • No account registration unless you email us for support.

5. Third Parties

  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
