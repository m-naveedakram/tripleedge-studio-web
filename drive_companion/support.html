Drive Companion

Help with Bluetooth, parking, media controls, Premium, ads, or privacy? Start here.

  • Connect your car — pair in Android Bluetooth settings, then pick the device in the Connect tab.
  • Music controls — enable notification access for Drive Companion in system settings so the app can read the active media session.
  • Parking spot — allow location when prompted; data stays on your device.
  • Weather — turn the weather card on in Settings; requires location and internet (Open-Meteo).
  • Premium / ads — Settings → Premium to purchase or restore. Free users see a labeled banner above the tab bar.
  • Ad privacy / consent — Settings → Privacy & data, or in-app ad privacy choices when available.
