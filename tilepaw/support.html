Tilepaw Support

Package: com.tripleedgestudio.tilepawgame

Gameplay

Drag the critter through every open tile without crossing the trail. Use undo or a hint if you get stuck. A voluntary rewarded ad can refill hints when none remain.

Tilepaw Plus

Create the one-time product tilepaw_plus in Google Play Console. Purchases remove ads. Use Restore in the app while signed into the Google account that made the purchase.

Ads and privacy

Debug builds use Google test ads. Release builds use production units. Use “Ad privacy choices” in the app where available.
