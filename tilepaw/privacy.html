Privacy Policy

Effective: November 5, 2026

TripleEdge Studio operates Tilepaw (package com.tripleedgestudio.tilepawgame). We do not sell personal information.

Game data

Level progress, stars, selected critter, sound preference and unfinished-level state are stored locally on your device. Tilepaw does not require an account and does not ask for contacts, location, photos, microphone or phone permissions.

Ads, consent and diagnostics

The free version uses Google AdMob for labeled banner ads, limited interstitial ads at completed-level breaks, and voluntary rewarded ads for hints. Google User Messaging Platform presents consent and ad privacy choices where required. Firebase Analytics measures bounded events such as app readiness and level starts/completions. Firebase Crashlytics receives crash diagnostics such as stack traces, device/app state and identifiers needed to diagnose failures.

Google services may process advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics and purchase status under their policies. See Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, and AdMob disclosure guidance.

Purchase

The optional one-time Google Play product tilepaw_plus removes ads. Google Play processes payment and returns purchase/entitlement status; TripleEdge Studio does not receive payment-card details.

Choices, retention and children

Ad privacy choices are available in the app. Clear app data or uninstall to remove local game data. Google controls retention for its services. Tilepaw is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13.
