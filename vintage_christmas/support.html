Vintage Christmas Postcard Maker

Last updated: November 28, 2026

Help for Vintage Christmas Postcard Maker by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.vintagechristmasjournal.

Making a postcard

  • Studio tab: design the front of your postcard with three tools: Photo & Film, Ephemera and Cardstock.
  • Photo & Film: choose a scene (Alpine Cabin, 1954 Hearth, Tin Express or Yuletide Cocoa), a film look and the amount of grain.
  • Ephemera: add stickers and drag them into place. Six stickers are free; the others unlock with a rewarded video.
  • Cardstock: pick a border (Deckled Cream, Kraft Parcel, Air Mail Stripes or Gold Foil Frame).
  • Flip the card to write your message, recipient and date, and choose a postage stamp, wax seal and postmark.

Scrapbook and exporting

Save a postcard to a day of your 25-day December scrapbook, then open the Scrapbook tab to revisit it. Export saves a PNG to your gallery under Pictures/VintageChristmasJournal, or use Share to send it through Android's share sheet. If an export does not appear, check that storage is available and try again.

Locked stickers and ads

Locked stickers show a lock. Tap one and choose “Watch to unlock” to unlock that sticker by watching an optional rewarded ad; unlocked stickers are kept on your device. If the video is not available, try again in a moment. A banner appears under the header and an occasional full-screen ad may appear after you save or export a postcard. Where required in your region, you can review your ad-consent choice under Settings → Ad privacy settings.

Sound

Turn the wax-seal, postmark and save sounds on or off under Settings → Sound effects.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear scrapbook to delete every saved postcard (unlocked stickers are kept). To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Vintage Christmas Postcard Maker → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Images already saved to your gallery or shared are not removed by this.
