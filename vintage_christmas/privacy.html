Privacy Policy

Vintage Christmas Postcard Maker

Last updated: November 28, 2026

TripleEdge Studio provides Vintage Christmas Postcard Maker (package: com.tripleedgestudio.vintagechristmasjournal), an offline vintage Christmas postcard studio and 25-day December scrapbook. This policy explains what the app stores, the advertising and analytics services it uses, and your choices.

1. No account, local-first

No sign-up or account is required. The postcards you save to your scrapbook (design choices, message, recipient and date text), the stickers you have unlocked and your sound setting are stored in private app storage on your device. We do not run a server that receives these items, and they are not uploaded to us. The app allows Android's standard app backup, so depending on your device's backup settings this local data may be copied to your own Google account backup and restored when you set up a new device.

2. Photos and your content

The app does not ask for access to your photos, gallery, camera, microphone or contacts. Postcards are built from the app's own scenes, film looks, borders, stickers and stamps, plus the text you type. What you type (message, recipient, date) stays on your device and is not attached to advertising or analytics requests.

3. Creating, saving and sharing postcards

Postcards are drawn on your device. When you export, the image (PNG) is saved to your gallery (Pictures/VintageChristmasJournal) and, if you choose Share, sent through Android's share sheet to the app you pick. Once a file is saved or shared, deleting a scrapbook entry cannot remove copies that already left the app.

4. Sound effects

Wax-seal, postmark and save sounds are generated on your device. The app does not record audio. You can switch sound effects off in Settings.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner under the header on every tab, an occasional full-screen interstitial after you save or export a postcard (never at launch or while editing, with a minimum gap of three minutes between full-screen ads, and not after every action), and optional rewarded videos that you start yourself by tapping “Watch to unlock” to unlock a sticker. Making postcards with the free stickers never requires watching an ad.

Before ads load, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form gathers the consent required in your region; where it applies, an Ad privacy settings entry in the app's Settings lets you review or change your choice at any time. The app requests ads with a maximum content rating of G. When the ad SDK runs, Google may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

6. Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand overall usage and Firebase Crashlytics (in release builds) to receive crash diagnostics so we can fix problems. Analytics events are limited to actions such as saving a postcard to the scrapbook (with the day number), exporting, sharing, and unlocking a sticker (with the sticker's ID); they contain no message text, names or images. These services may also process a device/installation identifier, the advertising ID, device model, OS version, app version, coarse region and crash stack traces. We use this only in aggregate to improve the app. See Firebase's privacy information.

7. Permissions

  • Internet / network state: load ads and send analytics and crash reports.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads and analytics SDKs.

Saving to your gallery on Android 10 and later needs no storage permission. The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location, notifications or body sensors.

8. Children

This app is a general-audience holiday greeting and journaling tool and is not directed at children. Ads are requested at a G maximum content rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Retention and deletion

Local items remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall. In Settings, Clear scrapbook permanently deletes every saved postcard (unlocked stickers are kept). To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Vintage Christmas Postcard Maker → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. This does not erase images you already saved to your gallery or shared, copies in your device backup, or data already processed by Google. Use Google's privacy controls for its data.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when functionality or data practices change. For privacy questions or requests about information you sent us, email us. Please do not include sensitive details you do not wish to share.
