Privacy Policy

Maithili Birthday Photo Frames

Last updated: November 17, 2026

TripleEdge Studio provides Maithili Birthday Photo Frames (package: com.tripleedgestudio.maithilibirthdayphotoframes), an offline Maithili birthday photo-frame and greeting-card maker. This policy explains what the app stores, the advertising service it uses, and your choices.

1. No account, local-first

No sign-up or account is required. The cards you make, your imported photos, saved drafts, favourite frames, unlocked frames and your chosen app language are stored in private app storage on your device. We do not run a server that receives these items, and they are not uploaded to us.

2. Photos

You add a photo using Android's system photo picker, so the app does not request access to your whole gallery and has no storage permission. A chosen photo is copied into the app's private storage and used only to render your card. Photos are not sent to our servers or attached to advertising requests.

3. Creating and exporting cards

Cards are drawn on your device. When you export, the image (PNG or JPG, 1080 × 1350 px) is saved to your gallery (Pictures/Sneh) and, if you choose Share, sent through Android's share sheet to the app you pick. Once a file is saved or shared, deleting the draft cannot remove copies that already left the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the screens (except the splash, language and onboarding screens), an occasional full-screen interstitial when you move from the editor to the preview (at most once every three minutes, and not while a 24-hour unlock is active), and an optional rewarded video that you start yourself by tapping “Watch video” to unlock all premium frames for 24 hours. Making cards with the four free frames never requires watching an ad.

Before ads load, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form gathers the consent required in your region. When the ad SDK runs, Google may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or any other analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own. The only third-party service it uses is Google AdMob (see section 4).

6. Permissions

  • Photos: you pick a single image through Android's system picker; no broad storage permission is used, and saving to your gallery needs no storage permission.
  • Internet / network state: load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads SDK.

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location, notifications or body sensors.

7. Children

This app is for people making birthday greetings and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Retention and deletion

Local items remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall. Delete individual drafts in My cards. To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Maithili Birthday Photo Frames → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. This does not erase images you already saved to your gallery or shared, or data already processed by Google. Use Google's privacy controls for its data.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when functionality or data practices change. For privacy questions or requests about information you sent us, email us. Please do not include photos or sensitive details you do not wish to share.
