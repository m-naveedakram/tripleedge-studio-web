Maithili Birthday Photo Frames

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Help for Maithili Birthday Photo Frames by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.maithilibirthdayphotoframes.

Making a card

  • Pick a frame from Home or Frames. In the editor you add a photo, switch frames, edit the text and add stickers, with undo and redo.
  • Photo: choose a photo from your device, then drag to position, pinch to zoom and rotate it.
  • Text: enter the name, an optional age and the Maithili wish, then choose a text colour and size. You can also copy or pick a ready-made Maithili wish from the Wishes tab.
  • Stickers: add stickers to your card.

Saving, exporting and sharing

Use Save to keep a card in My cards. Use Preview and export to save a PNG or JPG (1080 × 1350 px) to your gallery under Pictures/Sneh, or Share to send it through Android's share sheet. If an export does not appear, check that storage is available and try again.

Premium frames and ads

Four frames are free; the other premium frames are marked with a lock. Tap one and choose “Watch video” to unlock all premium frames for 24 hours by watching an optional rewarded ad. If the video is not available, try again in a moment. A banner appears at the top of most screens and an occasional full-screen ad may appear when you move from the editor to the preview, but not while an unlock is active. Where required in your region, a consent form is shown before ads load.

Languages

The app is available in English, Maithili and Hindi. You are asked once at the start, and you can change it anytime in Settings. The birthday wishes themselves are in Maithili.

Deleting your data

Delete individual drafts in My cards. To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Maithili Birthday Photo Frames → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Images already saved to your gallery or shared are not removed by this.
