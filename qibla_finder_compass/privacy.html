Privacy Policy

Qibla Finder & Compass

Last updated: November 16, 2026

TripleEdge Studio provides Qibla Finder & Compass (Android package com.tripleedgestudio.digitalcompassgps). This policy explains how the app uses device information and how you can control optional data collection.

1. Device sensors and offline tools

The Compass and Level use supported device sensors to calculate heading and tilt on your device. The Qibla tool calculates the direction toward the Kaaba from a device location or coordinates you enter. Magnetic compass readings, Level and Qibla calculations with manually entered coordinates can work offline. True north and live Qibla alignment depend on a usable location reference and supported sensors.

2. Location permission and sharing

The app asks for foreground location permission only when you choose Use current location. Android lets you allow approximate or precise access where supported. The app uses the available position to calculate Qibla, correct compass readings for true north, and show location details such as coordinates, accuracy, fix time and altitude when available. Approximate access can limit live alignment. The app does not request background location.

You can enter coordinates manually without granting location permission. If you choose to save them, the app keeps them in local app storage. You can remove them with Delete saved manual coordinates. The app uses coordinates on your device and does not pass them to AdMob, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics as app-supplied data. If you choose Copy or Share on the Location screen, coordinates are passed to the Android clipboard or to the app you select. Copies outside the app are controlled by you and the destination you choose.

3. Advertising and consent

The app uses Google AdMob for eligible banner ads. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether an advertising consent message or privacy options are required and presents them when applicable. If UMP makes privacy options available, reopen them under Settings → Privacy and ads → Privacy choices. Advertising choices are managed separately from Usage Analytics and Crash Reporting. The core tools are available without watching ads.

Google's ads SDK may process and share IP address, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID where available. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. IP-based estimates of general area are separate from coordinates you give the app. Google Mobile Ads data disclosure · Google Privacy Policy

4. Optional usage analytics and crash reporting

Usage Analytics and Crash Reporting are separate switches in Settings → Privacy & diagnostics, both off by default. If you turn on Usage Analytics, Firebase Analytics can send Google app usage events, app instance identifiers, device information and general location inferred from IP address to help us understand app usage. The app disables Firebase Analytics advertising ID collection.

If you turn on Crash Reporting, the release app can send saved crash reports to Google Firebase after you restart it. Crash reports can contain stack traces, device and app details, and installation identifiers. Crashlytics can store reports on the device while uploads are off. The app does not add precise coordinates or raw sensor readings to Analytics events or Crashlytics reports. Firebase data disclosure · Firebase privacy information

5. Your choices, retention and security

  • Change or revoke location access in Android's app permissions. Manual coordinates remain available.
  • Switch off either Firebase option in the app. Turning off Crash Reporting prevents new upload authorization at restart and requests deletion of unsent reports. Previously authorized transfers may still finish. Changing your choices does not delete data already sent to Google.
  • Delete saved coordinates in the app or clear the app's storage in Android Settings to remove its locally stored settings and saved coordinates. Uninstalling also removes the app's local storage. Copies you shared or saved elsewhere are managed separately.

Google processes service data under its applicable settings and policies. You can contact us about access or deletion requests. If you email us, we receive your email address and the details you send so we can respond. Please leave out precise coordinates and other sensitive information. Android app storage protects local settings and saved coordinates, but no storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

6. Children’s privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us so we can review the request. Parents and guardians can manage access to the app and its location permission through Android.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. Contact TripleEdge Studio with privacy questions or requests.
