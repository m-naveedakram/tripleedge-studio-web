Qibla Finder & Compass

Need help with Qibla Finder & Compass by TripleEdge Studio? This page supports Android package com.tripleedgestudio.digitalcompassgps.

Frequently asked questions

  • Why does the Compass seem inaccurate? Hold the phone steady and move away from metal, magnets, speakers and magnetic cases. If the app reports low accuracy, open its calibration help and follow the gentle figure-eight guidance. Compare readings away from interference.
  • Why is the Qibla pointer missing or moving? Confirm a fresh device location or enter valid manual coordinates on the Qibla or Location screen. Wait for a stable compass reading and turn slowly. The Qibla bearing can still be calculated from coordinates when live guidance is unavailable on a device without a usable compass sensor.
  • Why can’t the app get my location? Turn on Android Location, choose Use current location, and review the app's location permission in Android Settings. Approximate access can give a broader position and limit live alignment. If a fix is slow, try outdoors with a clearer view of the sky or enter your coordinates manually.
  • How do I use manual coordinates? Choose Use Manual Coordinates, enter latitude and longitude, and confirm. Save them only if you want to reuse them. Confirm your location again after moving. To remove a saved entry, use Delete saved manual coordinates.
  • What works offline? Magnetic Compass and Level use compatible device sensors. Qibla uses a current or manually confirmed position and is calculated on your device. Manual entry works without internet or location permission. Ads, ad privacy messages and Firebase transfers depend on connectivity.
  • Where are the advertising privacy choices? Open Settings → Privacy and ads. If Privacy choices is available, open it to review the advertising options Google provides for your situation.
  • How do I change analytics or crash reporting? In Settings → Privacy & diagnostics, change Usage Analytics and Crash Reporting independently. Crash Reporting applies to release builds and can send saved reports after a restart. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Report a technical issue

Include the app version, phone model, Android version, the affected screen, and the steps to reproduce the issue. If you attach a screenshot, remove precise coordinates, personal details and device identifiers.
