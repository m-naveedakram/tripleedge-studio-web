2. Tracing tips

  • Turn on Max brightness on open in Settings for the lightbox effect.
  • Use thin paper over your phone screen in a dim room.
  • Lock the art after positioning so it does not move while you draw.

3. Monetization (v1.0.2+)

  • Some capybara art is free; others unlock via a rewarded ad or Pro purchase.
  • Pro (capy_trace_pro) removes ads and unlocks all art permanently.
  • Use Restore purchases in Settings if you reinstall or change devices.

4. Data deletion

Android Settings → Apps → Capybara Art → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall.
