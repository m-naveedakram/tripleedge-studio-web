Privacy Policy

Kannada Happy Birthday Frames

Last updated: October 19, 2026

TripleEdge Studio provides Kannada Happy Birthday Frames (package: com.tripleedgestudio.kannadabirthdayframe), an offline Kannada birthday greeting-card maker. This policy explains what the app stores, the advertising and analytics services it uses, and your choices.

1. No account, local-first

No sign-up or account is required. The cards you make, your imported photos, saved drafts, exported-card records, favourite frames, unlocked frames, chosen language and any birthdays you add are stored in private app storage on your device. We do not run a server that receives these items, and they are not uploaded to us.

2. Photos

You add a photo using Android's system photo picker, so the app does not request access to your whole gallery and has no storage permission. A chosen photo is resized and copied into the app's private storage and used only to render your greeting card. Photos are not sent to our servers or attached to advertising or analytics requests.

3. Creating and exporting cards

Cards are drawn on your device. When you export, the image is saved to your gallery (Pictures/BirthdayBloom) and, if you choose Share, sent through Android's share sheet to the app you pick. Exported images carry no watermark. Once a file is saved or shared, deleting the draft cannot remove copies that already left the app.

4. Birthday reminders

If you add birthdays, they are stored on your device and a daily local check posts a reminder notification on the day. On Android 13 and later the app asks for notification permission when you add your first birthday. Reminders depend on Android settings and battery optimisation and may be delayed.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the browse screens, an occasional full-screen interstitial when you leave the editor after saving or exporting, and optional rewarded videos you start yourself to unlock premium frames. Core card-making never requires watching an ad.

Before any ad loads, a Google (UMP) consent form gathers the consent required in your region; where it applies, a Privacy options entry in the app's Settings lets you change your choice. When the ad SDK runs, Google may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The app requests ads with a maximum content rating of PG. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

6. Analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

Release builds use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand overall usage and to receive crash diagnostics so we can fix problems. These may process a device/installation identifier, the advertising ID, device model, OS version, app version, coarse region, in-app events and crash stack traces. We use this only in aggregate to improve the app. Collection is disabled in development builds. See Firebase's privacy information.

7. Permissions

  • Photos: you pick a single image through the Android photo picker; no broad storage permission is used.
  • Notifications (Android 13+): optional birthday reminders.
  • Internet / network state: load ads and send analytics/crash reports.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads and analytics SDKs.

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or body sensors.

8. Children

This app is for people making birthday greetings and is not directed at children. Ads are requested at a PG maximum rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Retention and deletion

Local items remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall. Delete individual drafts in My cards. To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Kannada Happy Birthday Frames → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. This does not erase images you already saved to your gallery or shared, or data already processed by Google. Use Google's privacy controls for its data.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when functionality or data practices change. For privacy questions or requests about information you sent us, email us. Please do not include photos or sensitive details you do not wish to share.
