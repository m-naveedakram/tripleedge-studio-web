Privacy Policy

GIF Maker — Photo to GIF

Last updated: September 16, 2026

TripleEdge Studio operates the mobile application GIF Maker — Photo to GIF (home-screen name: GIF Maker; package name: com.tripleedge.gifmaker). This policy explains what the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Summary

  • No account is required. We do not sell your personal information.
  • Photos you pick, GIF projects, captions, and export settings are processed on your device.
  • Exported GIFs are saved to your gallery only when you choose to export.
  • Free users may see ads (Google AdMob Open Bidding with Unity Ads and InMobi). Pro removes ads.
  • Where required, we show a Google UMP consent form before personalized ads.

2. What the App Does

  • Turn multiple photos from your gallery into an animated GIF
  • Reorder frames, adjust playback, motion, captions, and export quality
  • Save finished GIFs to your device gallery and share them through Android share sheets

3. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Editor state (frame order, timing, captions, theme, and default export preferences)
  • Pro purchase status (via Google Play, cached locally by the billing SDK)
  • Temporary encoded GIF files in app cache during export

We do not upload your photos or GIFs to our servers.

4. Permissions

GIF Maker uses the Android photo picker and scoped storage. It does not require broad storage read permissions on modern Android versions.

  • Internet & network state — ads, analytics, crash reporting, and opening legal links in your browser
  • Advertising ID — ad measurement where allowed by your consent choices
  • Google Play Billing — optional one-time Pro purchase
  • Notifications — optional idle reminder to create a GIF (you can deny notification permission)

The app does not use the camera or microphone unless you pick existing photos from your gallery.

5. Network Use

  • Google AdMob mediation (InMobi and Unity Ads may participate in Open Bidding; Unity may serve as a no-fill fallback)
  • Google Play Billing for Pro
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (anonymous usage and crash diagnostics)
  • Downloading Google Fonts used by the app UI (if not already cached)
  • Opening Play Store, privacy policy, terms, support, or website links in your external browser

6. Advertising, Consent & Advertising ID

If you are not a Pro user, the app may use Google AdMob and its mediation partners, including InMobi and Unity Ads, with Unity Ads as a client-side fallback when AdMob returns no fill.

These services may collect advertising identifiers, approximate location (derived from IP), device information, and interaction data to deliver and measure ads, subject to your consent where required.

We use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required. You can change ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and use in-app "Ad privacy choices" when available.

7. In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time Pro product (gif_maker_pro) through Google Play Billing. Pro removes ads and unlocks premium export features. Google processes payment; we do not store your card or bank details.

8. Analytics & Crash Reporting

GIF Maker uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for aggregated events and crash stacks. These services do not receive the contents of your photos or GIF frames.

9. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Account registration (name, email, or phone) unless you email us for support
  • Uploading your photo library to our servers
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, SMS, call logs, or calendar

10. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local project data remains until you clear app data or uninstall.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to respond.

To remove all app data: Android Settings → Apps → GIF Maker → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

12. Third Parties

  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob
  • InMobi
  • Unity Ads (no-fill fallback)
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics

13. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the "Last updated" date. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
