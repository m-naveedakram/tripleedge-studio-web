Tamil Happy Birthday Frames

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Help for Tamil Happy Birthday Frames by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.tamilbirthdayframe.

Making a card

  • Pick a frame from Home or Frames, or tap Create for a quick start, then add your details.
  • Photo tool: add a photo from your device, drag to position, pinch to zoom, rotate or flip, and adjust brightness and saturation.
  • Text tool: edit the name, Tamil heading and wish, pick a ready-made Tamil wish, choose a font and colour, and set text sizes.
  • Stickers: add up to 15 stickers, drag to place and resize them.

Saving, exporting and sharing

Drafts autosave on your device and appear in My cards. Use Export to save a PNG or JPG (720, 1080 or 1440 px) to your gallery, or Share to send it through Android's share sheet. Exports have no watermark. If an export does not appear, check that storage is available and try again.

Premium frames and ads

Some frames are marked with a lock. Tap one and choose “Watch video to unlock” to unlock it by watching an optional rewarded ad; the frame stays unlocked on your device. A banner appears at the top of the browse screens and an occasional full-screen ad may appear after you save or export. You can review your ad-consent choice under Settings → Privacy options when it is available in your region.

Languages

You choose the app language once after the splash screen, and you can change it anytime in Settings → App language. The birthday wishes themselves are in Tamil.

Birthdays and reminders

Add birthdays from the calendar icon to get a reminder on the day. Allow notifications when asked (Android 13 and later). Battery optimisation can delay reminders; reopen the app after force-stopping it.

Deleting your data

Delete individual drafts in My cards. To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Tamil Happy Birthday Frames → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Images already saved to your gallery or shared are not removed by this.
