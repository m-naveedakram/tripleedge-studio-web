Mochi Screen Pets

Need help with the overlay, Remove ads, or privacy? We're here.

Common fixes

  • Pet not on screen — Grant "Display over other apps", then turn the pet on from Home.
  • Pet disappeared — Open the notification or the app and start the pet again. Some phones stop overlays after a restart until you open the app.
  • Restore Remove ads — Settings → Mochi Pro → Restore purchases (same Google account).
  • Ads / consent — See our Privacy Policy and in-app Ad privacy choices.
