Privacy Policy

Mochi Screen Pets

Last updated: September 24, 2026

TripleEdge Studio operates Mochi Screen Pets (package: com.tripleedgestudio.shimejipets). We do not sell your personal information.

1. Summary

  • No account required. Pet settings stay on your device.
  • The overlay draws a companion pet on your screen only while you turn it on.
  • Free users may see Google AdMob ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded). Remove Ads hides ads.
  • We use Google UMP for consent where required.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics for anonymous usage and crash diagnostics.

2. Advertising

We use Google AdMob only (no third-party mediation networks). AdMob may use advertising identifiers and device data to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices. Banners are labeled and spaced away from buttons. Full-screen ads appear only after a natural break, such as the end of a focus session, and not on every tap. Rewarded videos play only when you choose them.

You can limit ad personalization in Android Settings → Google → Ads, and use in-app "Ad privacy choices" when available.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time Remove ads purchase (shimeji_remove_ads) via Google Play removes ads and unlocks Pro pets. Google processes payment; we do not store card details.

4. Permissions

  • Display over other apps — required to show the pet overlay, requested after you choose a pet.
  • Notifications — a persistent notice while the overlay service is running (Android 13+).
  • Advertising ID — used by AdMob for free users.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not upload your screen contents.
  • No account registration unless you email us for support.

6. Third Parties

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing
  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics
