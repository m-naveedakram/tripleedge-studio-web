Assamese Birthday Photo Frames

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Help for Assamese Birthday Photo Frames by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.assamesebirthdayphotoframes.

Making a card

  • Pick a frame from Home or Frames, or tap Create a card. The editor has Photo, Frame, Text and Stickers tools, with undo and redo.
  • Photo: choose a photo from your device, then drag to position, pinch or use the slider to zoom, rotate, flip or remove it.
  • Text: enter the birthday person's name and the Assamese wish, choose a text colour and adjust the text size. You can also copy or pick a ready-made Assamese wish from the Wishes tab.
  • Stickers: choose one finishing sticker for your card.

Saving, exporting and sharing

Use Save draft to keep a card in My cards (drafts can also be restored after the app closes). Use Preview & export to save a PNG or JPG (1080 × 1350 px) to your gallery under Pictures/Assamese Birthday Photo Frames, or Share to send it through Android's share sheet. If an export does not appear, check that storage is available and try again. If the app says the text is too long, shorten the name or wish.

Premium frames and ads

Four frames are free; the other ten are marked with a lock. Tap one and choose “Watch video” to unlock it by watching an optional rewarded ad; the frame stays unlocked on your device for 24 hours. If the video is not available, tap Retry in a moment. A banner appears at the top of the browse screens and an occasional full-screen ad may appear after you save or share a card. Where required in your region, you can review your ad-consent choice under Settings → Ad privacy options.

Languages

The app is available in English and Assamese. You are asked once at the start, and you can change it anytime in Settings. The birthday wishes themselves are in Assamese.

Deleting your data

Delete individual drafts in My Cards. To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Assamese Birthday Photo Frames → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Images already saved to your gallery or shared are not removed by this.
