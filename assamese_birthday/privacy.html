Privacy Policy

Assamese Birthday Photo Frames

Last updated: November 12, 2026

TripleEdge Studio provides Assamese Birthday Photo Frames (package: com.tripleedgestudio.assamesebirthdayphotoframes), an offline Assamese birthday photo-frame and greeting-card maker. This policy explains what the app stores, the advertising and analytics services it uses, and your choices.

1. No account, local-first

No sign-up or account is required. The cards you make, your imported photos, saved drafts, favourite frames, unlocked frames, your chosen app language and export format are stored in private app storage on your device. We do not run a server that receives these items, and they are not uploaded to us.

2. Photos

You add a photo using Android's system photo picker, so the app does not request access to your whole gallery and has no storage permission. A chosen photo is copied into the app's private storage and used only to render your card. Photos are not sent to our servers or attached to advertising or analytics requests. Sample portraits bundled with the app appear only in frame previews and never in your own card or export.

3. Creating and exporting cards

Cards are drawn on your device. When you export, the image (PNG or JPG, 1080 × 1350 px) is saved to your gallery (Pictures/Assamese Birthday Photo Frames) and, if you choose Share, sent through Android's share sheet to the app you pick. Once a file is saved or shared, deleting the draft cannot remove copies that already left the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the Home, Frames, Wishes, Settings and editor screens (never on the preview or onboarding screens), an occasional full-screen interstitial when you leave the export preview after saving or sharing a card, and optional rewarded videos that you start yourself to unlock premium frames; a rewarded unlock applies to that one frame for 24 hours. Making cards with the free frames never requires watching an ad.

Before ads load, a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form gathers the consent required in your region; where it applies, a Privacy options button in the app's Settings lets you review or change your choice at any time. When the ad SDK runs, The app requests ads with a maximum content rating of G. Google may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

5. Analytics (Firebase)

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand overall usage. Analytics stays switched off until the consent flow allows data collection. Once enabled it may process a device/installation identifier, the advertising ID, device model, OS version, app version, coarse region and in-app events. We use this only in aggregate to improve the app. The app does not use Firebase Crashlytics. See Firebase's privacy information.

6. Permissions

  • Photos: you pick a single image through the Android photo picker; no broad storage permission is used. On Android 8 to 9 (API 26–28), saving to your device uses the system document picker instead of a storage permission.
  • Internet / network state: load ads and send analytics.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ads and analytics SDKs.

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location, notifications or body sensors.

7. Children

This app is for people making birthday greetings and is not directed at children. Ads are requested at a G maximum content rating. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Retention and deletion

Local items remain until you delete them, clear the app's storage or uninstall. Delete individual drafts in My Cards. To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Assamese Birthday Photo Frames → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. This does not erase images you already saved to your gallery or shared, or data already processed by Google. Use Google's privacy controls for its data.

9. Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when functionality or data practices change. For privacy questions or requests about information you sent us, email us. Please do not include photos or sensitive details you do not wish to share.
