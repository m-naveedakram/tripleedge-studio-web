Help for Baby Kick Counter & Tracker by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.babykickcounter.

Movement concerns

If movements slow, stop or change, contact your midwife or maternity unit immediately. Do not wait for a count target or a support reply. This inbox is not a medical or emergency service. Read NHS movement guidance.

Sessions and history

  • Start a session, tap when you feel a movement, and pause or resume as needed. End, review and save the session.
  • Undo the latest event or review individual events. Deleting an event changes the count, not session duration.
  • Use the calendar date selector in History. If a session is missing from saved history, check for an unfinished session or pending review.
  • Reopening restores the persisted session. After a phone restart, interrupted timing may contain only confirmed duration.

Reminders

Allow notifications in Android Settings and check reminders in the app. Battery restrictions can delay them. Reopen the app after force-stopping it. Reminders are approximate, not medical monitoring.

Export and deletion

Select a date range in Export, choose PDF or CSV, and use Android's share sheet. Settings → Delete all data removes local records. There is no cloud restore; keep needed exports. Shared copies must be deleted separately.

Ads, purchases and languages

Current release ads and billing are disabled. Development builds may show Settings → Test ads. Ads require you to enable the switch, internet access and ad availability. Ads are blocked during unsaved sessions. Completing a rewarded ad grants a 24-hour ad break; closing it early does not.

The app supports English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch and Japanese. Change language in the app's language settings.
