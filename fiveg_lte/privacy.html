Privacy Policy

Effective: November 3, 2026

TripleEdge Studio operates 5G LTE (package com.tripleedgestudio.fiveglte). We do not sell personal information.

Information and processing

  • Network and SIM information: with optional Phone permission, the app reads carrier, subscription slot, radio technology and signal information supported by Android. IMEI, IMSI and SIM serial numbers are not collected.
  • Wi-Fi information: optional Location permission may allow Android to reveal the current Wi-Fi name. It is displayed in memory and is not sent to us.
  • Usage statistics: optional Usage Access lets the app calculate local data totals. Preferences and speed-test history are stored on the device; Android backup is disabled.
  • Speed tests: tests are user initiated and send test traffic to Cloudflare at speed.cloudflare.com, or a custom server selected by the user. That server receives the IP address and traffic needed to provide the test.
  • Notifications: optional notification permission supports usage alerts and reminders.

Ads, diagnostics and purchases

Google AdMob serves banner and occasional interstitial ads. Google User Messaging Platform presents consent and privacy choices where required. Firebase Analytics provides aggregate app-usage measurement and Firebase Crashlytics receives crash diagnostics such as device/app state and stack traces. An optional one-time Google Play product (fiveg_lte_remove_ads) removes ads; Google Play processes payment and returns purchase status to the app.

Depending on region, Google services may process advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, interactions, diagnostics and purchase information under their own policies. See Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, and AdMob data disclosure guidance.

Choices and retention

Permissions are optional and can be revoked in Android Settings. Ad privacy choices are available in app when required. Use Clear local data or uninstall to remove local preferences and history. Google controls retention for its services under its policies.

Children and security

The app is a general-audience network utility and is not directed to children under 13. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission is completely secure.
