Help for Telugu Birthday Photo Frames by TripleEdge Studio. Package: com.tripleedgestudio.telugubirthdayframes.

Making a card

  • Pick a frame from Home or Frames. The editor opens with four tools: Photo, Frame, Text and Stickers.
  • Photo: choose a sample portrait or upload your own photo, then drag to position, pinch or use the slider to zoom, rotate, flip or reset.
  • Text: edit the recipient name, optional age (1–120), the Telugu wish and the sender name, and pick a text colour. You can also choose ready-made Telugu wishes from the Wishes tab.
  • Stickers: add up to 12 stickers and drag them into place.

Saving, exporting and sharing

Use Save Draft to keep a card in My Cards. Use Preview & Export to save a PNG or JPG (1280 × 1600 px) to your gallery under Pictures/Telugu Birthday Photo Frames, or Share to send it through Android's share sheet. If an export does not appear, check that storage is available and try again. If the app says the text is too long, shorten the name, wish or sender.

Premium frames and ads

Some frames are marked with a lock. Tap one and choose “Watch Video” to unlock it by watching an optional rewarded ad; the frame stays unlocked on your device. If the video is not available, tap Retry in a moment. A banner appears at the top of the browse screens and an occasional full-screen ad may appear after you save or share a card.

Languages

The app is available in English and Telugu. You are asked once at the start, and you can change it anytime in Settings → App Language.

Deleting your data

Delete individual drafts in My Cards. To remove everything, use Android Settings → Apps → Telugu Birthday Photo Frames → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Images already saved to your gallery or shared are not removed by this.
